# Mythic Duel — Cards v0

This document defines the cards, shrines, ancestors, and decks for Version 0 of Mythic Duel.

# Deck A: Red Baobab

**Identity**: Hunter / Spirit / Memory / Evolution Midrange.

## Red Baobab Shrines

### Shrine of the Red Baobab
- Shrine
- Vitality: 10
- Effect: The first time each turn one of your Beings dies, gain 1 Memory.

### Hunter’s Root-Stone
- Shrine
- Vitality: 10
- Effect: Your Hunters get +1 Attack while attacking Shrines.

### Moonlit Roots
- Shrine
- Vitality: 10
- Effect: At the end of your turn, if you did not attack a Shrine this turn, restore 1 Vitality to a Shrine.

## Red Baobab Ancestors

### First Hunter
- Ancestor (Hunt)
- Awaken condition: One of your Beings destroyed an enemy this turn.
- Awaken cost: 2 Memory.
- While Awakened: Your Beings get +1 Attack while attacking Shrines.

### Grandmother Beneath the Roots
- Ancestor (Root, Moon)
- Awaken condition: You restored a Shrine this turn or skipped Shrine attacks last turn.
- Awaken cost: 2 Memory.
- While Awakened: At the end of your turn, restore 1 Vitality to your most damaged Shrine.

### Child Who Remembered the Dead
- Ancestor (Memory, Spirit)
- Awaken condition: A Spirit Dispersed since your last turn.
- Awaken cost: 2 Memory.
- While Awakened: The first time each turn you gain Memory, draw 1 then discard 1.

# Deck B: Iron Hyena — Pack Pressure

**Identity**: Aggressive Shrine pressure / high Pack density / damaged Shrine payoffs / blocked attack bleed.

## Shrines (Shared Iron Faction)

- **Iron Hyena Den**: Vitality 10. First time each turn one of your Beings attacks a Shrine, gain 1 Memory.
- **Bone-Fang Shrine**: Vitality 10. Your Beings get +1 Attack while attacking damaged Shrines.
- **Rusted Moon Shrine**: Vitality 10. Whenever an enemy Spirit disperses, deal 1 damage to an enemy Shrine.

## Pack Pressure Ancestors

### Ancestor of the First Pack
- Ancestor (Iron, Hyena, Pack)
- Awaken condition: You declared a Pack attack this turn.
- Awaken cost: 2 Memory.
- While Awakened: The first time each combat a Pack attack is blocked, deal 1 damage to the attacked Shrine.

### Bone-Splitter Mother
- Ancestor (Hyena, Earth, Pack)
- Awaken condition: A Being became Wounded or survived combat this turn.
- Awaken cost: 2 Memory.
- While Awakened: Your Wounded Pack Beings get +1 Attack while attacking Shrines. The first time each turn a Wounded Pack Being attacks a Shrine, deal 1 damage to that Shrine.

### Smith Who Passed the Fang
- Ancestor (Iron, Relic)
- Awaken condition: You played a Relic this turn.
- Awaken cost: 2 Memory.
- While Awakened: Your first Relic each turn costs 1 less. When a Pack Being with a Relic dies, you may move that Relic to another ready Pack Being on your battlefield.
