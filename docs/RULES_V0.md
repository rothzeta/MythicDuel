# Mythic Duel — Rules v0

## Project identity

Online-first PvE/roguelite card battler.

```text
Shrines are sacred objectives.
Cards carry mythic progression.
Spirits Disperse into Memory.
Ancestors guide playstyle.
Beings evolve through deeds, tags, and combat.
```

## Not in v0

```text
No Shrine evolution.
No Sanctuary Pool.
No Support Pieces.
No Consequences.
No Favor counters.
No path counters.
No live Reactions.
No UI.
```

## Win condition

Each player starts with 3 named Shrines.

Each Shrine has:

```text
10 Vitality
persistent damage
one simple passive/triggered effect
```

If all 3 of a player's Shrines are broken, that player loses.

## Resources

### Offerings

```text
Offerings increase by 1 each of your turns.
Offerings refresh at the start of your turn.
Cap: 8.
```

Used for:
- Beings
- Relics
- Rites
- Tactics
- normal card costs

### Memory

Memory persists until spent.

Generated by:
- Disperse
- Shrine effects
- Being death effects
- Rites
- combat effects

Used for:
- Possessions
- Ancestor awakening
- evolution costs
- Recall costs
- some Tactics/Rites

## Turn structure

```text
1. Ready
2. Resource
3. Draw
4. Main 1
5. Combat
6. Main 2
7. End
```

## Draw model

```text
Start hand: 5.
At end of turn, keep up to 1 card.
Discard the rest.
At start of turn, draw until you have 5 cards.
When deck is empty, shuffle discard into deck.
Forgotten cards do not return.
```

## Game Zones & Card Destinations

Cards in play and in hand move between different zones depending on their type and status.

### Zones

- **Hand**: At the end of a turn, players may keep 0 or 1 card in hand. The rest are moved to Discard.
- **Battlefield**: Beings, Spirits, and Tokens stay in play on the Battlefield until removed by combat, card effects, or evolution.
- **Attached**: Relics and Possessions stay attached to their host unit until displaced, destroyed, or the host leaves the battlefield.
- **Prepared**: Prepared Tactics stay prepared in their face-down zone until triggered or expired.
- **Lineage**: Ancestors reside in the Lineage zone and are never discarded or forgotten.
- **Shrines**: Shrines stay in play and are never discarded or forgotten.

### Discard (The recoverable pile)

Cards go to Discard when they are:
- Discarded from hand (at the end of turn)
- Used/resolved (for Rites and Tactics without the Fade keyword)
- Destroyed (for Beings and Tokens)
- Dispersed (for Spirits)
- Replaced by evolution (the host card of an evolution, unless it has Ascend)
- Detached normally (Relics or Possessions when host is removed)

When a player's deck is empty, the Discard pile is reshuffled to form a new deck. Cards in Discard remain part of the battle's memory.

### Forgotten (The gone pile)

Cards go to Forgotten when they are:
- Fade cards after resolving (Rites or Tactics)
- Mythic cards leaving the battlefield
- Consumed evolution hosts with the Ascend keyword
- Replaced prepared tactics when the prepared slots are full

Forgotten cards do not reshuffle back into the deck and normally cannot be used or recovered.


## Phase / initiative rule

A phase contains 4 player-turns.

```text
Phase 1: Red → Iron → Red → Iron
Phase 2: Iron → Red → Iron → Red
Phase 3: Red → Iron → Red → Iron
Phase 4: Iron → Red → Iron → Red
```

At the end of each phase, turn order reverses.

Each player can have up to 2 Prepared Tactics.

Prepared Tactics do not expire by default. They stay prepared until they trigger, are replaced, or a card effect says otherwise.

If both Prepared slots are full and you prepare another Tactic, you choose 1 of your currently prepared Tactics to replace. The replaced card moves to Forgotten and does not trigger.

## Combat

Each combat starts with 2 Standard Assault slots.

Pack can expand this bandwidth.

At the start of combat, count your ready Pack Beings.

For each ready Pack Being you control, gain 1 Pack Assault slot, to a maximum of 5 total attacks this combat.

A Standard Assault slot may be used by any legal attacker.

A Pack Assault slot may only be used by a legal attacker with Pack.

If a Pack attacker attacks while a Pack Assault slot is available, it automatically spends a Pack Assault slot first.

If no Pack Assault slot is available, a Pack attacker may still use a Standard Assault slot if one remains.

The attacker declares each attack target and attack order one attack at a time as each attack begins resolving, not all at once before combat starts.

Each attack resolves separately:

```text
Attack → Defend → Resolve
Attack → Defend → Resolve
```

### Attack targets

Beings can attack:
- enemy Beings
- enemy Spirits
- enemy Shrines

Spirits can attack:
- enemy Beings
- enemy Spirits

Spirits cannot attack Shrines unless a card says otherwise.

### Defending

If a Shrine is attacked, defender may block with one ready Being.

If a Being or Spirit is attacked directly, it is the defender. No extra blocker is chosen.

### Damage

Attacker and defender deal damage equal to Attack.

Damage to Beings and Spirits clears at end of turn.

Damage to Shrines is persistent.

## Keywords / States

### Mythic

Mythic cards represent unique legendary ascensions.

A Mythic card:

- cannot be played normally from hand
- can only enter the battlefield through evolution
- has Presence 2 unless the card states otherwise
- is Unique: a player cannot control more than one copy of the same Mythic card
- when it leaves the battlefield, move it to Forgotten instead of discard

### Unique

A Unique card cannot coexist with another copy of the same card controlled by the same player.

Rules:

- Mythic cards are Unique by default
- named L3 cards are Unique
- L2 cards are not Unique unless the card says so

### Presence

Each player has 5 Presence slots.

Beings, Spirits, and Tokens consume Presence.

Default unit Presence cost: 1.

Default Mythic / L3 Presence cost: 2.

Relics and Possessions consume 0 extra Presence while attached.

Shrines, Ancestors, Prepared Tactics, hand, deck, discard, and Forgotten consume no Presence.

A player cannot play, summon, or evolve a unit if doing so would exceed their Presence limit.

On evolution, the host is replaced first for Presence purposes.

### Disperse

When a Spirit would die, remove it from battlefield, gain 1 Memory, then put it into discard unless the card says otherwise.

### Possession

A Possession is a Spirit attached to a Being.

```text
A Being can have only 1 Possession.
If a second Possession attaches, the old Possession Disperses.
If the host dies, the Possession Disperses.
```

### Prepared Tactic

During Main 1 or Main 2, pay the cost and place a Tactic face-down.

```text
Max 1 active Prepared Tactic per player.
When its trigger happens, it reveals and resolves automatically.
If it does not trigger by the end of opponent’s next turn, discard it or Forgotten if it has Fade.
```

### Pierce

When this unit attacks a Shrine and is blocked, if it deals fatal combat damage to the blocker, excess damage is dealt to the original Shrine target.

### Wounded

If a Being or Spirit takes non-fatal damage, it becomes Wounded.

A Wounded unit deals 25% less combat damage.

```text
Wounded damage = floor(Attack × 0.75), minimum 1.
```

Wounded clears at the end of that unit controller’s next turn.

### Guard

Enemy attacks must target a Guard Being before attacking Shrines, if able.

### Ambush

This unit may attack enemy units the turn it enters.

Ambush does not allow attacking Shrines the turn it enters unless stated.

### Evolve

Some evolution cards can be played normally for full cost.

L2 evolution cards may usually be played normally unless the card says otherwise.

L3 / Mythic evolution cards cannot be played normally. They can only enter the battlefield through evolution.

If the player controls a matching lower form and the evolve condition is met, the player may play the evolution card over that Being for its Evolve cost.

A card may only evolve into a higher level:

- L1 -> L2 allowed
- L2 -> L3 allowed
- L2 -> L2 forbidden
- L3 -> L2 forbidden
- L3 -> L3 forbidden unless a special rule explicitly allows it

When a Mythic card leaves the battlefield, it moves to Forgotten instead of discard.

When evolving:

```text
replace old card with new card
move Relics and Possessions to evolved card
move damage unless card says otherwise
old card goes to discard
```

Some evolution conditions are checked through deed markers on the lower form.

Common deed markers:

- HasDestroyedEnemy
- HasDamagedShrine
- HasBrokenShrine

When a unit performs one of these deeds, it keeps that marker while it remains in play.

When a unit evolves, the evolved form inherits the deed markers of the lower form.

### Fade

After this card resolves, move it to the Forgotten zone instead of discard.

If a Prepared Tactic with Fade expires without triggering, move it to Forgotten instead of discard.

Forgotten cards do not return when discard is shuffled into deck.

### WeaponMaster

This unit has Pierce while it has a Relic attached.

### SoulSlayer

This unit has Pierce while it has a Possession attached.

### Pack

Pack units unlock Pack Assault slots at the start of combat based on ready Pack Beings.

Pack Assault slots are reserved for legal Pack attackers only, and Pack attackers automatically consume Pack Assault slots before Standard Assault slots when both are available.

## Ancestors

Ancestors live in Lineage.

They are visible to both players.

They do not enter battlefield.

Each has:
- Awaken condition
- Awaken cost
- Awakened effect

Only one Ancestor can be Awakened at a time.

If a new Ancestor awakens, the previous Awakened Ancestor becomes Dormant.

## Seats, factions, and decks

Seat is runtime position.

Faction is content identity.

Deck is the constructed package loaded into a seat.

The simulator resolves games as Seat 1 vs Seat 2, even when the loaded decks are mirrors or share a faction.
